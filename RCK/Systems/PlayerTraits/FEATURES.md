# RCK.PlayerTraits features

RCK.PlayerTraits adds compatibility player traits for weapon mods, ammo limits, knockback, armor durability, movement, map access, and rapid-fire behavior.

## Traits

| Trait ID | Display name | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Accuracy_Modder` | Accuracy Modder | Your projectile weapons receive an Accuracy Mod when you get them. |
| `Ammo_Amateur` | Ammo Amateur | Your projectile weapons get 25% more max ammo. |
| `Ammo_Artiste` | Ammo Artiste | Your projectile weapons get 50% more max ammo. |
| `Ammo_Auteur` | Ammo Auteur | Your projectile weapons get double max ammo. |
| `Ammo_Stocker` | Ammo Stocker | Your projectile weapons receive an Ammo Capacity Mod when you get them. |
| `Blinker` | Blinker | When damaged, you blink a short safe distance away, with a brief cooldown. |
| `Immovable` | Immovable | You ignore forced knockback and gain vanilla knockback resistance. |
| `Kleistagnosia` | Kleistagnosia | Accepted for compatibility with older player characters; it has no effect on its own. |
| `Knockback_Peon` | Knockback Peon | You take half normal knockback. |
| `Mapless` | Mapless | You cannot open the minimap or quick-teleport map. |
| `Melee_Maniac` | Melee Maniac | Your melee attacks use the vanilla Fast Melee trait. |
| `Melee_Maniac_2` | Melee Maniac + | Your melee attacks use the stronger vanilla Fast Melee trait. |
| `Myrmicapo` | Myrmicapo | Your armor takes half durability damage from hits. |
| `Myrmidon` | Myrmidon | Your armor takes only a quarter durability damage from hits. |
| `Pants_on_Autofire` | Pants on Autofire | Your projectile weapons become rapid-fire when you get them. |
| `Rate_of_Fire_Modder` | Rate of Fire Modder | Your projectile weapons receive faster-firing hardware when you get them. |
| `Remise_Beast` | Remise Beast | Your melee weapons become rapid-fire when you get them. |
| `Silencerist` | Silencerist | Your projectile weapons receive a `Silencer` when you get them. |
| `Trigger_Happy` | Trigger Happy | Your gun cooldowns are cut to 80% of normal. |
| `Trigger_Junkie` | Trigger Junkie | Your gun cooldowns are cut to 60% of normal. |

## Legacy and related names

Legacy `InHead=false` player traits are accepted by the campaign converter when possible. NPC Interactions player-language traits are documented in RCK.Interactions.

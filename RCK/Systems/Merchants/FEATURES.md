# RCK.Merchants features

RCK.Merchants lets campaign makers turn custom NPCs into shops, adjust prices and stock, and use non-cash payment traits. Inventories are original item pools built from the trait names, documented CCU behaviour (facts only), and vanilla shop behaviour.

Multiple merchant-type traits merge their item pools. Stock modifiers change duplicate listings, stack quantities, durability, or charges. Cost-scale traits change normal cash prices for goods and services.

## Custom currencies

`Banana_Barter` charges 1 Banana. `Booze_Bargain` charges 1 Beer, Whiskey, or Cocktail. `Blood_Covenant` charges 20 HP, and `Pound_of_Flesh` charges 50 HP; both refuse a purchase that would drop the buyer to 0 HP. `Swap_Meet` trades the buyer's cheapest eligible loose item.

`Swap_Meet` never takes equipped, quest, undroppable, character-exclusive, 0-value, money, key, key-card, safe-combination, Mayor Badge, property deed, phone, or voucher items. Guns and armor are traded as whole items.

The shop window and interaction buttons show the payment in words, such as "1 Banana", "1 Booze", "20 HP", "50 HP", or "1 Item". Bribes, muggings, deportation bribes, and free-item vouchers keep their vanilla behavior.

## Legacy names

Accepted from older content and converted where possible: `Hacker`, `Shopkeeper`, `Soldier`, `Thief`, `Vampire`.
